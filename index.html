Before we begin

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Pilates experience?

No. Private sessions are suitable for beginners as well as experienced practitioners.

What should I expect in the first session?

We will discuss your goals, observe how you move, and begin with exercises that help establish a thoughtful foundation.

Can sessions be adapted for prenatal, postnatal, or return-to-movement goals?

Sessions can be thoughtfully modified based on your needs. If you have a medical condition or injury, please follow guidance from your healthcare professional and share any relevant restrictions before we begin.

Do you offer packages?

Yes. Regular private sessions and packages are available depending on your goals and preferred location.
